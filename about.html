About RAAP Engineering

RAAP Engineering is centered on Mark Gibson's senior systems leadership and a company focus on reverse engineering, fixture-development, and additive manufacturing support for mission-focused customers.

Operating Focus

Mission-oriented support with an emphasis on rigor and trust.

RAAP Engineering is oriented toward organizations that need disciplined engineering thinking, clear written communication, and experienced technical judgment across government, defense, and industrial work.

The company is actively shaping a sustainment-focused story around reverse engineering, fixture development, and additive manufacturing support for parts that are obsolete, unavailable, or difficult to source through normal procurement channels.

Manufacturing Focus

Additive manufacturing as a practical answer to unavailable legacy parts.

RAAP Engineering supports conversations around 3D printing and additive manufacturing for replacement parts, fixtures, and hard-to-find components when original suppliers are no longer available or lead times are operationally disruptive.

That positioning fits defense, maritime, aerospace, and industrial sustainment environments where speed, domestic responsiveness, and technically credible communication matter as much as the part itself.

Founder Leadership
